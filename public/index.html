HOW TO PLAY

Goal: survive 10 depths. Each player sees only their station clue. Talk, compare readings, and commit the exact joint action sequence before the timer expires. Wrong sequences cost 25% hull.

Some readings are marked [STATIC] and can be wrong. After depth 3, free chat jams and only 12 tactical pings remain. In optional Saboteur mode, one hidden human gets corrupted intelligence and the crew votes after each depth.

Team value: DEAD RECKONING turns concise relays, confirmation, and shared decision-making into visible habits for friends, classrooms, and remote-team icebreakers.

No accounts, database, paid APIs, or external AI calls are required.
